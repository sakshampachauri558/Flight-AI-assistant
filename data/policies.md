# Airline Policies

## Cancellation Rebooking Rule
If an airline-caused flight cancellation occurs:
The customer may choose:
1. Free rebooking on the next available flight within 24 hours
OR
2. Full refund

The customer chooses between these options.

## Delay Compensation Rule
For a delay:

### Delay under 3 hours
Provide:
* ₹500 meal voucher

### Delay more than 3 hours
Provide:
* Meal voucher
* Lounge access

### Delay more than 5 hours
Provide:
* Meal voucher
* Lounge access
* Hotel accommodation

Important:
Hotel accommodation covers **only the delayed hours**, not a full night's stay.

## Refund Processing Rule
For airline-caused cancellations:
* Refund is full.
* Processing occurs within 7 business days.
* Refund must go to the original payment method.

The agent must not offer another payment method.

## Fare Difference Rule
If a customer voluntarily chooses a higher-fare flight:
* Customer must pay the fare difference.
* Agents cannot waive a fare difference above ₹1,500 without supervisor approval.

Therefore:
Fare difference <= ₹1,500 may be handled according to the applicable policy.
But:
Fare difference > ₹1,500 requires human/supervisor escalation.

## Loyalty Tier Rule
Gold and Platinum customers receive:
* Priority rebooking
* First access to next-available seats

However:
**Gold and Platinum customers do NOT automatically receive additional compensation beyond the standard policy.**
